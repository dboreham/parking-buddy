Building

Interior dimensions. Lengths accept decimal feet or feet and inches, e.g. 9'6".

Doors

Offset is measured from the left end of the front/back wall, or the front end of a side wall. Doors marked auto are spaced evenly along their wall; type an offset to place one yourself.

Vehicles

Vehicles are parked in list order: pinned ones first, quick-access ones last (nearest the doors). Drag a parked car on the plan to pin it there; scroll or press R over it to rotate.

Obstacles

Posts, benches, stairs: rectangles from the front-left interior corner.
